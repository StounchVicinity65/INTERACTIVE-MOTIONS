〰 Oscillatory Motion 〰 দোলন গতি
Definitionসংজ্ঞা

Oscillatory motion is any motion that repeats back and forth about an equilibrium position. The restoring force always points toward the centre. Key parameters are amplitude (maximum displacement) and frequency (oscillations per second).
